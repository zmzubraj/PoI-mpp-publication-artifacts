## Notes

- The Task 20 reporting layer emits SVG, CSV, and JSON outputs. The older PDF/placeholder paths are not the current publication contract.
- `F1`-`F4` are conceptual manuscript diagrams and remain outside the quantitative completeness gate. The older conceptual files named `F5` and `F6` are supplemental-only to avoid collision with canonical quantitative F5 and F6.
- E5 has T10 and no canonical figure. F9 and F10 both derive from E6.
- The E8 raw input for Task 20/22 is now the path-free `POI_MPP_E8_PUBLICATION_ARTIFACT_V3` JSON emitted by the production publication replay, plus `configs/confirmatory/e8.yaml`.
- Task 22 manual review authenticity now depends on an external detached signature plus a trusted allowed-signers file; the bundle itself cannot self-certify review independence.
- Task 22 bundle manifests and reports now carry explicit states: `CANDIDATE_VERIFIED` for candidate bundles without a sentinel and `FROZEN_VERIFIED` for frozen bundles that have passed destination reverify and then received the sentinel.
- `results/frozen/<run_id>/MPP_ARTIFACT_COMPLETE` is reserved for a future complete freeze and must not exist for the current August 25, 2026 workspace state.
