## Frozen claim specification

The bounded first-publication claim is not that PoI invents a new primitive for
optimistic dispute, committed open-weight inference, zero-knowledge inference,
or weighted consensus in isolation. The candidate differentiator is narrower:

- one committed model execution;
- post-commit execution and semantic evidence surfaces;
- challenge/data-availability retention;
- receipt-to-credit-to-next-epoch-weight lifecycle; and
- local EVM/Foundry and E1-E8 artifact discipline.

## Main findings

- `CommitLLM` is the closest implementation-level predecessor for committed
  open-weight inference plus challenge-time audit disclosure.
- `opML` directly overlaps the optimistic fraud-proof path for machine-learning
  inference on blockchain.
- `EigenAI` overlaps deterministic inference plus optimistic public auditing and
  economic enforcement.
- `zkGPT`, `SVIP`, `TensorCommitments`, `Communication-Efficient Verifiable
  Attention`, `NanoZK`, and `VeriLoRA` show that verifiable LLM inference is an
  active and crowded adjacent surface rather than an empty novelty lane.
- `Optimistic TEE-Rollups` weakens any stronger claim that the broader
  AI-plus-blockchain composition is uniquely new, even though confidential TEE
  execution is outside the frozen first-publication scope.
- `Hollow-LLM Attack` is defeating evidence against any simplistic argument that
  proof of inference correctness alone necessarily binds computational effort.
- US11494677B2, US11562228B2/US11983608B2, US11303454B2, and
  US20250165967A1 show pre-existing patent overlap around blockchain-recorded
  inference, efficient blockchain-native ML verification, inference-event
  provenance, and smart-contract-mediated model verification/transaction.
- IEEE 3127-2025 and active blockchain-consensus/provenance projects show that
  blockchain/AI auditability and consensus-framework terminology are not empty
  standardization lanes, although they do not establish PoI's exact lifecycle.
